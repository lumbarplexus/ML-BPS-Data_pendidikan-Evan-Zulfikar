Clustering and Prediction of Education Indicators

Abstract: This study applies K-Means and HDBSCAN clustering algorithms to group provinces based on 9 social indicators and 5 education target indicators. Subsequently, Linear Regression and Random Forest models predict education outcomes using the social indicators. Spearman correlation analysis validates predictor-target relationships. Results are presented with comprehensive metrics including silhouette scores, mean absolute error, R-squared values, and feature importance rankings.
